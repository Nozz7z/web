Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 3, 2025

Cmdix is a terminal tool for Android developed by Nozz Trick. This application is designed to run local shell commands on your device without collecting or transmitting any personal data.

1. Data Collection

Cmdix does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), usage analytics, or any kind of user data.

2. Permissions

The app may request permissions to access certain device features for command execution. These are required strictly for functionality, and no data is sent externally.

3. Third-Party Services

Cmdix does not integrate with any third-party analytics or advertising services.

4. Offline Operation

The app works completely offline. No internet connection is required or used by Cmdix for its main functionality.
